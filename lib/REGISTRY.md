# Behaviour registry

One line per behaviour in `hub.js`. A pattern may declare a behaviour in its
`behaviours:` header field and hook it with `data-hub-module="<name>"` only if
the name is listed here — CI enforces both directions.

**Delivery is out of the pattern's hands.** A site may carry this file as its
own asset and reference it with a single `<script type="module">` tag, or a
platform may inject it. A pattern never carries a tag itself and never knows
which arrived; the
hooks are inert data attributes until the platform injects the library on
served pages. Behaviours must therefore always be pure enhancements — the
no-JS render is the page.

Each release publishes the built bundle at
`/hub-behaviours/v1/hub.min.js`, and at an immutable
`/hub-behaviours/<version>/hub.min.js` beside it. The version is
`window.HubBehaviours.version` and it moves under the rules in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#the-bundles-version-and-what-publishing-does-with-it).

| Behaviour | Since | What it does | Markup contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| `menu` | v1.4 | Makes every parent item of a navigation list an operable control with `aria-expanded`: click and `Enter` toggle its panel, `ArrowDown` opens it and moves into it, `Escape` closes it and returns focus, `ArrowLeft` and `ArrowRight` move between parents, an outside press closes, and hover opens with a short grace before it closes. A parent that is a real link keeps the link and gains a button beside it; a parent with no `href` becomes the button. A panel whose edge would leave the viewport is anchored to its other edge, at either side: one that hangs from its end edge and would leave by the start edge, as on a parent a wrapped row puts first on its line, is anchored to its start edge (v1.14.1). Acts only while the stylesheet positions the child list, so the same list inline in a drawer is left as authored; with no library the panels still open on hover and `:focus-within` from the stylesheet. Emits `hub:menu:open` and `hub:menu:close`; it moves nothing itself | `data-hub-module="menu"` on a `<nav>` whose first list is the menu; a parent is any item of that list holding a list of its own, and its control is its first `<a>` or `<button>` |
| `overflow` | v1.4 | Folds the items that do not fit a menu's row into one last item, in the brand's own word, and unfolds them in order when room returns; measures on every resize and once the fonts have arrived. Items are moved, never cloned. Acts only while the list's stylesheet sets `--hub-overflow: more` on it, so a rung or a width that wraps or scrolls is left alone; with no library the list is whatever the stylesheet made it. The folded item is a parent like any other, so `menu` opens it. Emits `hub:overflow:change` with the number folded | `data-hub-module="overflow"` on a `<nav>` whose first list is the menu; optional `data-hub-overflow-label` (default `More`) |
| `shrink` | v1.4 | Sets `data-hub-shrink="scrolled"` on a bar once the page has scrolled past a threshold and removes it above, so the bar's own stylesheet can tighten; the threshold is the bar's full height unless an option says otherwise, and is never re-read from the shrunk state. Acts only while the stylesheet has the bar sticky or fixed. Emits `hub:shrink:compact` and `hub:shrink:full`. Any transition is the stylesheet's, and reduced motion is the stylesheet's to honour | `data-hub-module="shrink"` on the bar; optional `data-hub-shrink-after` in pixels |
| `counter` | v1.5 | Counts a figure up from nought as it comes into view, keeping the authored prefix, separators, decimals and suffix, and ends on the authored text exactly; text holding no number is left alone; does nothing under reduced motion, so the authored figure is the page. Does nothing on a block that sets or inherits `--hub-motion: none` (v1.12). Emits `hub:counter:done` on each figure | `data-hub-module="counter"` on a `<dl>` (every `<dt>` counts) or on the figure itself; `data-hub-counter` marks any other element inside; optional `data-hub-counter-duration` in milliseconds (default 1200) |
| `scrollspy` | v1.5 | Marks the contents link whose heading the reader passed last with `aria-current="true"` and `.hub-scrollspy-current`; nothing is current above the first heading; nothing moves. Emits `hub:scrollspy:change` | `data-hub-module="scrollspy"` on a `<nav>` whose links are hashes to headings with ids on the page; optional `data-hub-scrollspy-offset` in pixels (default 96) |
| `carousel` | v1.5 | Builds a previous and a next control inside the block. A radio group of slides moves by checking the next input and wraps; a sideways scroller moves by one item and the controls disable at the ends. With no library the block is what was authored, which already moves by radio, swipe or keyboard. Smooth only without reduced motion. Emits `hub:carousel:change`. **Where the controls sit is `data-hub-carousel-controls`** (v1.6): `under`, the default, a labelled row beneath the rail that holds one place as the rail moves; `edges`, a pair of round controls over the rail's left and right, each keeping its label as its accessible name with an arrow drawn in place of the words; `top` (v1.11), the pair at the end edge above the rail from `48rem`, moved below it on a phone; `none`, no controls at all, leaving swipe and keyboard: a sideways scroller shows no scroll bar, so on a scroller `none` leaves a mouse no way along it. A word it does not know is read as `under`, and `edges` and `top` need a block that holds still, so where the block is itself the scroller they resolve to `under`. **`data-hub-carousel-look`** (v1.11): `round` draws each control as a chevron in a 44px circle, its word kept as the accessible name, and takes the pair away while the rail has nothing to scroll. **`data-hub-carousel-phone`** (v1.11): `hide` leaves a phone to swipe, below `48rem`. Each setting is read from its attribute first and then from the matching custom property on the block (`--hub-carousel-controls`, `--hub-carousel-look`, `--hub-carousel-phone`), so a pattern's variant class can set it; a block with none of them builds the controls it built before 1.11. The placement in force is written to `data-hub-carousel-controls-used`, so what a page ended up with is readable rather than guessed at | `data-hub-module="carousel"` on the block holding the radio inputs, or on the scroller or its parent; optional `data-hub-carousel-prev-label`, `data-hub-carousel-next-label`, `data-hub-carousel-controls`, `data-hub-carousel-look` and `data-hub-carousel-phone`, or the three custom properties |
| `marquee` | v1.3 | Moves a rail along by itself and builds the control that stops it; clones the run once so the loop is seamless, with the copies hidden from assistive technology and out of the tab order; halts on hover, on focus, while a visitor drags it, and when it scrolls out of view. Does nothing at all under reduced motion, so no control appears either. Does nothing on a block that sets or inherits `--hub-motion: none` (v1.12), except where a pattern leaves it unset on a rung that is itself a choice to move. With no library the block is the rail it was authored as. `data-hub-marquee-look` or `--hub-marquee-look` set to `round` (v1.11) draws the control as a pause icon, a play triangle once pressed, its word kept as the accessible name. `data-hub-marquee-fit` or `--hub-marquee-fit` set to `still` (v1.13) leaves a run that already fits its row as authored, with no copies, no control and no movement, and starts it the first time a narrower screen leaves it too long to fit; without it a run that fits is doubled and glides part way, as before. Keeps its place in fractions of a pixel, so a slow glide on a fast screen still moves (v1.12.3). Emits `hub:marquee:paused` and `hub:marquee:resumed` | `data-hub-module="marquee"` on the block whose own list is the scroller; optional `data-hub-marquee-speed` in pixels per second (default 30), `data-hub-marquee-pause-label`, `data-hub-marquee-look` and `data-hub-marquee-fit` |
| `signup` | v1.10.0 | Turns a `signup-card` (or the older `signup-steps` block) into one question at a time: a back control, a segmented progress bar and a count, one step showing (two side by side from `60rem`), each checked before the next with the reason said in words. "Looking for" takes several answers, summed as the join flow reads them. Adds two interest steps from the brand's own labels, capped at six picked, and a first-name field. A tapped single answer moves on by itself; a keyboard never does. The date of birth jumps box to box as it is typed, or becomes three scroll wheels; the two can be split by width, one below `60rem` and the other from it, a date given at one width kept at the other, and the year wheel can open at an age with day and month still blank (v1.14), and a complete date shows the age and star sign, the age, or nothing. Live members of the brand, from the platform's member search, show from arrival and narrow to who the visitor is looking for once they say: faces in the card (on `signup-steps`, a row beside it when wide); nothing shows when there are none or the search fails. A line under the progress bar gathers the answers so far (v1.8), in lower case in English and with the answers' own capitals in any other language (v1.12.2). Given places, it asks where the visitor lives straight after "looking for" - as far as the page's scope leaves open: country, region, then a town: a list to pick from where the region has twelve or fewer, otherwise every town from the first tap, biggest first, in a scrolling list narrowed as it is typed; regions grouped by nation where the platform's names carry one, the largest first; in the USA the ZIP code first, the lists a tap away (`-postal`: `first`, `lists`, `off`), sent as `zipCode` - narrows the members to the answer, widening a level at a time when they are few, and sends the town's `lat` and `long` from the places files published beside the bundle; places that cannot be reached drop the step (v1.9). After each answer a short line under the answers so far, at random and never twice in a visit, from the lines published beside the bundle (English; per interest only for the card's own platform) and the page's own; a page in another language uses only its own (v1.10); going back takes it away until the next answer (v1.12.1). The last step builds the hand-off URL by hand, as the platform writes every join link and then the answers on top: each parameter the visitor arrived with, the page's `pn` attribution and, where the page's language differs from the brand's, `culture`; interests `;`-separated, spaces as `%20`, email Base64 (v1.7.1) and goes to the brand's join link after a drawn tick. On a phone each step brings the card to the top of the screen; on a wide screen only when the step's button would fall below the fold. A page can set the order and grouping of the questions, `-screens` and `-screens-wide` from `60rem` (`iam seeking | location | dob intent | enjoy | email`): a question it leaves out is neither asked nor sent, and the join flow asks it; a grouping that names an unknown step or one twice, or puts email anywhere but the last screen, is ignored for today's; on a card with its own order the members wait for "looking for" to be answered, wherever it is asked, unless it is fixed or ticked from "I am" (v1.15). Motion only without reduced motion. With no library the card is the authored form. Emits `hub:signup:step` and `hub:signup:handoff` | `data-hub-module="signup"` on a `signup-card` or `signup-steps` whose fieldsets carry `data-hub-signup-part` = `iam`, `seeking`, `dob`, `email`, where a hidden `mt` or `lf` input may stand in for `iam` or `seeking` (that question is then skipped and the value sent); optional settings and words, every one listed with its values and default in `patterns/signup-card/settings.json` (held to this bundle by `ci/check_signup_settings.py`) |
| `reveal` | v1 | Entrance animation as content scrolls into view; staggers direct children when `data-hub-reveal-children` is present; does nothing under reduced motion; does nothing on a block that sets or inherits `--hub-motion: none` (v1.12), which is how a pattern's still rung stays still, and a page whose styles never set it moves as before | `data-hub-module="reveal"` on the element, optional `data-hub-reveal-children` on a container |
| `drawer` | v1.2 | Closes a `<details>` drawer on `Escape` and on a press on its backdrop, and holds the page still behind it while it is open; with no library the drawer still opens, closes and takes the keyboard, because the disclosure is doing that. Modal only while the stylesheet has the panel fixed to the viewport, so the same markup at a width where the menu is an ordinary row is left alone. Emits `hub:drawer:open` and `hub:drawer:close`; it moves nothing itself | `data-hub-module="drawer"` on a `<details>` whose `<summary>` is the control and whose other element child is the panel; the backdrop is that `<details>`'s own `::before` |
| `tabs` | v1.1 | Builds a tablist from the panels' own labels and shows one panel at a time, with roving `tabindex`, arrow keys, Home and End; no tab control ships in the markup, so with no library every panel renders stacked under its own heading. Emits `hub:tabs:change`. Panel entrance is skipped under reduced motion | `data-hub-module="tabs"` on the panels container, `data-hub-tab-label="…"` on each direct-child panel, optional `data-hub-tabs-label` naming the tablist |
