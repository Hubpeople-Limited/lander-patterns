The campaign page that is one screen long: a photograph filling the window,
one claim over it, the join control, a line of reassurance and one piece of
real proof. The header carries no menu and nothing follows but the footer, so
the visitor has one thing to do.

It suits paid traffic that arrived with one promise in mind and a brand with a
photograph worth the whole screen. Before that photograph arrives, the page
shows the library's marked placeholder in its place.

It does not argue. There are no reasons, no voices and no questions; a
promise that needs those is a promise for the page that proves first.
