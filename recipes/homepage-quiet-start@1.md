The second, gentler way through the no-photography page, with the opening claim
centred on the ordinary page background rather than held on a dark ground. The
first screen is calm, and the four tiles below it are what a visitor actually
reads.

It suits a brand whose strength is what it offers rather than how loudly it can
say so - a members' club, a niche community, anything where the tone should be
reassuring rather than urgent. It is also the safer of the two no-photography
pages on a brand whose colour is very strong, because that colour appears once,
at the end.

It does not make an entrance. If the first screen has to stop somebody
scrolling, the bolder version of this page puts the claim on a dark ground and
does exactly that.
