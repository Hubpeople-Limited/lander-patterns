The front page that leads with a picture rather than a sentence. One image
fills the first screen with the promise laid over it, the steps that follow are
image cards rather than text, and the page closes on the brand colour so the ask
is the one flat surface in a page made of photographs.

It suits a brand whose pictures are its best argument, and only that brand. The
opening image is seen at full size on a laptop and it carries the first
impression on its own, so it has to be genuinely good and it has to have people
in it who agreed to appear.

It does not explain much. There is no room for a paragraph anywhere on this
page, and a brand with a proposition that needs one is better served by an
opener made of words. It also asks the most of a photography budget of any page
in this menu.
