The front page that leads with a picture rather than a sentence. The first
screen is the brand's best photograph - filling the screen with the promise
over it, or held large above it, decided from the picture itself - the steps
that follow are image cards rather than text, and the ask at the foot is the
one flat surface in a page made of photographs.

It suits a brand whose pictures are its best argument, and only that brand. The
opening image is seen at full size on a laptop and it carries the first
impression on its own, so it has to be genuinely good and it has to have people
in it who agreed to appear.

It does not explain much. There is no room for a paragraph anywhere on this
page, and a brand with a proposition that needs one is better served by a
first screen made of words. It also asks the most of a photography budget of
any page in this menu.
