The safety page for a brand that has everything and wants it all on one page.
The opener is dark, the promises underneath are set one to a screenful with
nothing to hide behind, the protections follow categorised, and then the actual
policies appear as plain links a reader can go and read.

It suits a brand that will be asked hard questions - by a payment provider, an
app store, a journalist, or a member who has had a bad experience. Every claim on
it is checkable, which is the only kind of safety page worth having when somebody
is checking.

It does not work half-built. The promises set large are unforgiving of vague
wording, and the link section is dishonest without real published policies behind
every one of those links. A brand still writing them should take the short safety
page and come back.
