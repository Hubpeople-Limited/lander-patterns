```recipe
opens: a claim, then real writing rather than a grid of features
closes: a full-width band in the brand colour, carrying the one control
signature: claim, prose, the steps, compliance marks, band
pairing: brand
notes: written as an explanation; the steps say what happens after a report
```

The safety page as something a person wrote. After the opening claim it is a
piece of prose - proper paragraphs, in the brand's voice - followed by what
happens step by step when something goes wrong, then the compliance marks, then
the ask.

It suits a brand whose safety story is a process rather than a feature list: a
moderation team, a review, a human at the end of it. Explaining that in cards
loses it, and this shape has room for the sentence that actually reassures
somebody.

It does not scan well. A visitor skimming for the word "verification" will find
it slower here than on a page of cards, and writing this page properly takes
someone an afternoon rather than filling in a grid.
