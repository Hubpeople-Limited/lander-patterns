```recipe
recipe: article-long-read@1
shape: narrative
shell: article-longform
grounds: plain, plain, plain, soft, plain, soft, brand
opens: the headline and byline over a picture, then a contents list
closes: the writer named on a quiet panel, then the band
signature: bylined header, contents, the piece, a lifted line, author
pairing: brand
notes: for a piece past about fifteen hundred words
```

The long read: everything the ordinary article page has, plus a contents list a
reader on a phone can jump from and one line lifted out of the writing partway
down. The lifted line breaks up what would otherwise be a long run of
paragraphs.

It suits a substantial piece - a real investigation, a personal account, a guide
worth the length. The contents list is not decoration on a page this long: it is
the difference between a reader scrolling and a reader leaving.

It does not suit a short piece. A contents list over four headings looks like an
apology for the article's length, and there is no summary box here, so somebody
who wanted the answer in ten seconds will not find it.
