```recipe
recipe: features-what-you-get@1
shape: peer-set
shell: features
grounds: plain, soft, plain, soft, brand
opens: decide it from the material - the first screen says what the brand does: the first capability itself, a plain claim naming it, or a member's words
closes: decide it from what the page has said - one visible way to act at the foot: a band, a line after the questions, or a single link
signature: panels, the table, questions
pairing: brand
notes: for the capabilities the homepage has no room for
```

The page that lists what the brand does, at the length the homepage cannot
afford. The capabilities as full-width panels that climb from light to the
brand colour to dark as a reader goes down, a table showing which plan includes
what, and the questions that follow; how the page opens on them and how it asks
at the foot are decided from the material.

It suits a brand with more than four things worth naming, or one whose support is
regularly asked whether the site does a particular thing. The panels give each
capability a whole band, which is the right amount of room for one that needs a
sentence of explanation.

It does not replace a homepage. Nobody arrives here first, and it should be
linked from the navigation rather than promoted. A brand with three features has
a homepage section, not a page.
