The how-to page: takeaways at the top, the guide itself with one captioned
picture inside the writing, and the follow-up questions answered at the foot.
The picture goes where a picture genuinely helps, and the writer is named last.

It suits a piece somebody arrives at from a search with one question in mind -
how to write a first message, what to do about a match who has gone quiet. The
questions at the foot are the ones the guide leaves behind, and answering them
there is what stops a reader going back to search for them.

It does not work without a picture worth captioning. A stock image dropped into
the middle of a guide is worse than no image at all, and if there is nothing real
to show, the plain explainer page is the better shape.
