
The features page for a visitor who is comparing rather than exploring. The dark
centred opener says plainly that the page is going to lay the plans out, the
panels are read as evidence rather than as a tour, and the table underneath is
what the page is really for.

It suits a brand whose free and paid tiers are close enough that the difference
needs stating carefully, and one confident enough to state it. The order still
puts the panels first, so somebody who does not yet know what the site does is
not dropped straight into a table.

It does not welcome anybody. Opening on a dark ground with a comparison promise
is the wrong first impression for a visitor arriving from an advertisement, who
should land on a homepage instead.
