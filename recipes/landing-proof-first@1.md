The paid campaign page that proves before it asks: the promise beside a
photograph, the reasons to believe it as tiles, three real people saying it
in their own words, and the ask. The header carries the logo and the join
control and no menu, so every link on the page is one the page chose.

It suits a brand with a photograph worth leading with and three testimonials
it can stand behind, sending paid traffic that arrived with one promise in
mind. The page answers that promise and nothing else.

It does not explain. There is no how-it-works, no pricing and no questions
answered; a visitor who wants those is a visitor for the homepage, and the
footer still carries the way there.
