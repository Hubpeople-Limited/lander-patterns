The safety page as a straight answer: the promise on the brand colour, the
protections as cards, and an ask at the foot. Each card names what the protection
is, what it does and what that means for the reader, and the page closes by
asking because on a dating brand somebody reading about safety is usually
deciding whether to join.

It suits a brand whose protections are real features that can be named:
verification, moderation, blocking, reporting. Three screens is all it takes, and
a short honest page is read where a long one is skimmed.

It does not go into detail. There are no policy links, no explanation of what
happens after a report, and no compliance marks. A brand under scrutiny, or one
whose protections are a process rather than a list, needs one of the longer
safety pages.
