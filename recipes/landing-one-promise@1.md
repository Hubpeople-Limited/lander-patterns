The campaign page that says one thing: the claim on the brand colour with
nothing but the join control under it, the reasons as tiles, three real
people, and the ask again. No photograph, so it is the landing page a brand
can build on its first day and the one for a promise that is stronger as
words than as a picture.

It suits a brand sending paid traffic to a single, specific claim - a place,
a price, a kind of person - and a brand with no photography yet. The header
carries no menu, so the claim is the only thing to do.

It does not set a scene. A visitor who needs to see the brand before they
believe it wants the page that proves first.
