The same index of a brand's writing, opened as a statement rather than a label.
The claim sits centred on a dark ground and names what the brand writes about
rather than announcing that it has an archive, the pieces follow as ruled rows,
and the topics sit on a tint underneath so they read as a second way in rather
than as a footer.

It suits a brand whose writing is part of the offer — where somebody arrives
for the advice and joins afterwards, rather than the other way round. The dark
opener gives the page a front of its own, which matters when it is the page
people link to and land on.

It does not suit a thin archive, and it does not suit a brand whose other pages
all open dark. Two pages on one site opening the same way is the repetition
this layer exists to avoid, so where the homepage already leads on a dark
ground, take the plain index instead.
