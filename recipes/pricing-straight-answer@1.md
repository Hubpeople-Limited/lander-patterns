```recipe
shape: reference
shell: pricing
grounds: plain, soft, plain, brand
opens: decide it from the plans - the first screen states what it costs with no build-up: the plans themselves can be the first screen, or a plain statement above them
closes: decide it from what the page has said - a band, a line after the questions, or a single link
signature: tier cards, questions
pairing: brand
notes: the price is above the fold and nothing is withheld
```

The pricing page that answers the question the visitor arrived with. What it
costs on the first screen, the plans as cards side by side, the questions that
come up at the point of paying, and the ask. Somebody who came only to find out
what it costs can leave satisfied in about fifteen seconds.

It suits a brand with two or three clear plans that differ on an obvious number,
and a price it is comfortable showing. It is the default pricing page and the
one to reach for unless there is a reason not to.

It does not sell. There is no case made before the number, no comparison of what
each plan includes in detail, and no trust marks. A brand whose price looks high
without an argument in front of it wants the page that argues first.
