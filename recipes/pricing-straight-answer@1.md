```recipe
recipe: pricing-straight-answer@1
shape: reference
shell: pricing
grounds: plain, soft, plain, brand
opens: a plain statement of what it costs, with no build-up in front of it
closes: a full-width band in the brand colour, carrying the one control
signature: stated opener, tier cards, questions, band
pairing: brand
notes: the price is above the fold and nothing is withheld
```

The pricing page that answers the question the visitor arrived with. A short
stated opener, the plans as cards side by side, the questions that come up at
the point of paying, and the ask. Somebody who came only to find out what it
costs can leave satisfied in about fifteen seconds.

It suits a brand with two or three clear plans that differ on an obvious number,
and a price it is comfortable showing. It is the default pricing page and the
one to reach for unless there is a reason not to.

It does not sell. There is no case made before the number, no comparison of what
each plan includes in detail, and no trust marks. A brand whose price looks high
without an argument in front of it wants the page that argues first.
