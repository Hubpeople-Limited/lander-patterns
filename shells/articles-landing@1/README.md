# articles-landing@1

Generated by `ci/compose.py` from `ci/page-recipes.json` - never hand-edit; change the source and regenerate.

A **articles-landing** page shell. The index of a brand's writing: a stated opener, the pieces as ruled rows rather than a grid of cards, and the topics as plain links. No photography anywhere, because an index rarely has one image per entry that is worth having.

Fetch `page.html` and `page.css`, write real copy into the slots, duplicate whatever block a pattern's comments say to duplicate, strip every comment, and keep each `{{ }}` token exactly as found - the platform fills those. The first and last content sections are empty in `page.html`: they are the page's opening and its close, decided from the material, and the default this shell was designed with for each sits beside the page in `opening-default.html` and `closing-default.html`, ready to paste in where the page can say why.

## Wiring

The brand's stylesheet loads first: link the brand's own `global.css` - its token values, per [TOKENS.md](../../TOKENS.md) - ahead of `page.css`, or merge `page.css` into it unchanged. `page.css` carries only the patterns' rules and reads every colour, radius and font from the brand's tokens.

The page carries its own site footer: this shell closes with the `colophon` pattern, and the platform fills its legal links and footer menu at serve time - keep every `{{ }}` token in it exactly as found. A site header is likewise a pattern a page carries (`masthead-nav` in this library); this shell ships without one.

## Regions

This shell is a frame. What it settles: the head, the site header, the `<main>` landmark round the content, the order of the sections, the footer, and the responsive behaviour every pattern carries. What it leaves open, and ships empty in `page.html`:

- **The opening** - the first gap. `hero-stated` is the default this shell was designed with; it is in `opening-default.html`, its CSS already in `page.css`, and it goes into the gap only where the page can say why. Otherwise the page opens on another opener from INDEX.md, on the content's own first row, on a real member's words, or on nothing above the content at all. The recipe's `opens:` line says what the first screen has to do; the material decides what does it.
- **The closing** - the last gap. `cta-band` is the default, in `closing-default.html`, and it goes in only where the page can say why. Otherwise the page closes on a line in the prose, a single link, a quiet panel or the last row of its content. The recipe's `closes:` line says what the foot has to do; the page decides what does it, and every page keeps one visible way to act.
- **The middle's density** - how many items, a line or a paragraph each, one section or three - is the material's, within the patterns' stated needs.

## What is still yours to decide

Copy, imagery, brand - and the three regions above. The words in every slot are the brand's own; images are real material meeting each pattern's stated needs; the look comes from the brand's token values ([TOKENS.md](../../TOKENS.md)). To change the frame itself - the sections between the regions and their order - build from patterns directly instead.
