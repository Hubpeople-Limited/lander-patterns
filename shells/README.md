# Shells

Pre-assembled page shells - generated by `ci/compose.py` from `ci/page-recipes.json`, never hand-edited.
Each shell is a frame: the head, the site header, the `<main>` landmark, the order of the sections and the footer are settled. Its first and last content sections are regions - shipped as defaults, decided per page - and the middle's density is the material's; each folder's README says what may replace what. Fetch one folder, decide the regions, write real copy into the slots, strip the comments.

- **homepage-conversion@3** - homepage - masthead-nav -> hero-split -> steps-plain -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - Opener, how it works, the questions, the ask - the ordinary conversion homepage.
- **homepage-no-photography@3** - homepage - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=deep -> heading-block -> benefit-tiles -> cta-band -> colophon - The homepage for a brand with no photography. heading-block is not decoration here: benefit-tiles opens at h3 and says so in its own markup, so without an h2 above it the page skips a heading level.
- **homepage-photo-led@2** - homepage - masthead-nav -> hero-overlay -> steps-numbered -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - The photography-led homepage: a cinematic full-screen opener, the sign-up flow as image cards, the questions, the ask. Nearly every live lander leads with imagery; this is the shell for them. Image slots take real material or the library's lib/placeholders/ stand-ins while the brand's photography is still coming.
- **homepage-photo-editorial@2** - homepage - masthead-nav -> hero-centred -> zigzag-rows -> faq-details -> cta-image -> colophon - The editorial photography homepage: the claim held above a wide image band rather than over it, alternating image-and-copy rows carrying the argument, and a photographic close. The image-led variant for a brand whose register is considered rather than cinematic.
- **pricing@3** - pricing - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> pricing-tiers -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - State the offer, show the tiers, answer the objection, ask.
- **pricing-comparison@1** - pricing - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=deep -> comparison-table -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - The pricing page as a table of criteria rather than a row of tier cards - the shape for a brand whose tiers differ on many small things rather than on one headline number.
- **pricing-value@1** - pricing - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=brand -> offer-split -> pricing-tiers -> trust-row -> cta-band -> colophon - The pricing page that argues before it prices: the case for paying, then the tiers, then the marks that say the brand is legitimate. For a brand whose price needs a reason first.
- **safety@3** - safety - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=brand -> safety-protections -> cta-band -> colophon - On a dating brand this is a conversion page in disguise, so it closes like one.
- **safety-explained@1** - safety - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> prose-column -> steps-plain -> trust-row -> cta-band -> colophon - The safety page written as an explanation rather than a grid - prose, then what happens step by step, then the compliance marks. For a brand whose protections are a process rather than a list of features.
- **safety-detailed@1** - safety - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=deep -> claim-stack:ground=plain -> safety-protections -> link-cluster:ground=soft -> cta-band -> colophon - The long safety page: the promises set large, then every protection categorised, then the policies themselves as real links. For a brand with the material to be specific.
- **article-explainer@1** - article - masthead-nav -> article-masthead -> key-takeaways -> prose-column -> source-note -> author-note -> cta-band -> colophon - The ordinary advice article: the summary a reader gets without reading, the piece, where its figures came from, and who wrote it. The default shape for editorial on a dating brand.
- **article-longform@1** - article - masthead-nav -> article-masthead -> article-toc -> prose-column -> pull-quote -> prose-column -> author-note -> cta-band -> colophon - The long read: contents at the top so a phone can navigate it, the writing broken part-way by one line lifted out of it, and the writer named at the foot. Two runs of body copy, because a line lifted out of the text has to have text on both sides of it. For a piece past about fifteen hundred words.
- **article-guide@1** - article - masthead-nav -> article-masthead -> key-takeaways -> prose-column -> article-figure -> prose-column -> author-note -> cta-band -> colophon - The illustrated guide: takeaways at the top, then the writing with a captioned picture set inside it rather than after it, and who wrote it at the foot. The shape for a how-to a reader arrives at with one question and needs shown as well as told.
- **landing-one-screen@1** - landing - masthead-nav:nav=minimal -> hero-squeeze -> colophon - The one-screen campaign page: a menu-free header, then a photograph, one claim, the join control, the reassurance under it and one piece of real proof, with nothing below it but the footer. For paid traffic where the only question is whether the visitor signs up.
- **articles-landing@1** - articles-landing - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> listing-rows:ground=soft -> link-cluster:ground=plain -> cta-band -> colophon - The index of a brand's writing: a stated opener, the pieces as ruled rows rather than a grid of cards, and the topics as plain links. No photography anywhere, because an index rarely has one image per entry that is worth having.
- **features@1** - features - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> feature-panels -> comparison-table -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - What the brand actually does, as a page: the claim, the feature panels climbing the ground ladder, a table comparing what each tier includes, and the questions that follow. The page a partner asks for when the homepage cannot carry every capability.
- **features-table@1** - features - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=deep -> comparison-table -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - The features page as a comparison: the claim on a dark ground, then straight into the table of what each tier includes, then the questions. For a visitor who arrived to compare rather than to explore - the table is the page, and nothing stands between the opener and it.
- **location@1** - location - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> prose-column -> heading-block -> member-grid -> source-note -> link-cluster:ground=soft -> cta-band -> colophon - A page for one place, and the first shell whose main block is written by the platform rather than by the page. The prose above the members is the part no competitor can copy and the part an agent cannot invent; source-note under the grid is where the honest caveat about what the block shows goes; link-cluster carries the sibling places, which is what stops a set of these reading as isolated doorways.
- **locations-landing@1** - locations-landing - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> listing-rows:ground=soft -> cta-band -> colophon - The index above a set of location pages. Rows rather than cards, because a place has no photograph of its own that is honest to use, and because the set grows: a brand adds a region and adds a row. It exists so a set of location pages is a structure with a parent rather than a run of pages reachable only from a search result.
- **landing@1** - landing - masthead-nav:nav=minimal -> hero-split -> heading-block -> benefit-tiles -> testimonial-grid -> cta-band -> colophon - The paid campaign page: the header carries no menu, so every link on the page is one the page chose; the split opener, the reasons as tiles under the h2 heading-block supplies (benefit-tiles opens at h3 and says so in its own markup), three real voices, the ask. For traffic that arrived from an advert with one promise in mind.
- **landing-stated@1** - landing - masthead-nav:nav=minimal -> hero-stated:ground=brand -> heading-block -> benefit-tiles -> testimonial-grid -> cta-band -> colophon - The one-promise campaign page, for a brand with no photograph worth leading with: the claim stated on the brand colour under a menu-free header, the reasons as tiles, three real voices, the ask.
- **about@1** - about - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> portrait-prose -> claim-stack:ground=soft -> cta-band -> colophon - The founder page: a plain statement of who is behind the brand, the person and their story with the portrait given room, the promises the story earns set large, the ask. The page every small brand has and the one a narrative shape was made for.
- **about-values@1** - about - masthead-nav -> hero-stated:ground=plain -> claim-stack:ground=soft -> portrait-prose -> cta-band -> colophon - The about page led by what the brand stands for: the promises before the person, then the founder and their story as the evidence for them. For a brand whose values are the point and whose founder is the proof.
- **location-photo@1** - location - masthead-nav -> hero-band:ground=plain -> prose-column -> heading-block -> member-grid -> source-note -> link-cluster:ground=soft -> cta-band -> colophon - A page for one place that opens on a photograph: the place's own words, its members, the honest caveat about what the block shows, the sibling places and the ask. The worded location shell with a band of photograph in place of the stated opener, so a brand with a photograph of the place or its people has a second shape for the page.
- **locations-photo@1** - locations-landing - masthead-nav -> hero-band:photo=behind,ground=plain -> listing-rows:ground=soft -> cta-band -> colophon - The index above a set of location pages, opening on a photograph of the brand's people with the title on a panel in front of it, then the places as ruled rows and the ask. The rows stay words, because a place has no photograph of its own that is honest to use; the one photograph is the brand's, not a place's.
- **homepage-portrait@1** - homepage - masthead-nav -> hero-portrait:ground=plain -> heading-block -> member-grid -> steps-plain -> cta-band -> colophon - The people-led homepage: one large portrait beside the claim and the join control, the brand's own members under a heading, how joining works, the ask. The shell for a brand whose first screen should show a face; the sign-up card takes the join button's place when the page starts the sign-up itself.
- **homepage-quote@1** - homepage - masthead-nav -> hero-split -> steps-plain -> quote-image -> faq-details -> cta-band -> colophon - The conversion homepage with a member's voice in it: the opener, how joining works, one real quote over a photograph, the questions, the ask. The quote sits after the steps, where the page has said how it works and a voice earns a pause.
- **about-quote@1** - about - masthead-nav -> hero-band:ground=plain -> prose-column -> quote-image -> cta-band -> colophon - The about page told in the brand's words and then a member's: a band of photograph over the title, the brand's own account, one real quote over a photograph, the ask. For a brand with no founder to put forward and one quote that says what it is like to belong.
